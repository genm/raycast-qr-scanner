# Governance

QR Scanner is currently a single-maintainer project. `@genm` is the project maintainer and has final responsibility for scope, reviews, releases, security coordination, and community moderation.

Routine changes are decided through issues and pull requests. Significant changes to permissions, privacy behavior, supported platforms, native boundaries, licensing, or distribution require a public design discussion before implementation. Security-sensitive details may be handled privately until coordinated disclosure is safe.

At least one approving maintainer review and all required CI checks are expected before merging. The maintainer may appoint additional maintainers after sustained, trusted contributions and will record that change here and in `CODEOWNERS`.

If the project becomes inactive, the maintainer may mark it unmaintained, archive it, or transfer it to a willing successor. Any transfer must preserve the MIT license, security-reporting records, repository history, release provenance, and access to distribution channels. Archival must disable automation that could still publish releases or imply active support.
